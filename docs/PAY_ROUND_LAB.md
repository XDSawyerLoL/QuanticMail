# Experimental single-height view change

Separate synthetic protocol domain. Four fixed hybrid authorities; threshold three; at most one Byzantine authority; authenticated messages, fixed membership, and honest no-rollback state assumed. Two illustrative values only, not actual wallet transactions. This is an executable model, not a complete or proven production BFT implementation.

Prepare votes do not settle a transfer. A prepare certificate needs three hybrid votes in the same round. A commit vote requires a locally approved prepare and a verified prepare certificate; the authority retains that certificate while emitting its commit vote. Finality requires three matching hybrid commit votes and the prepare certificate. Changing view is monotonic and invalidates local old-round voting. Three signed change reports must agree on target view. Highest reported prepared certificate forces the next proposal; conflicting equal-height certificates are rejected. Without a reported prepared certificate the model permits either synthetic value. A genuine 2-2 PREPARE split can therefore move to a new round; it cannot be used to unlock old single-phase circulation votes. An old final certificate can coexist only with a matching safe value under the stated assumptions and correct honest behavior.

The tests exercise 2-2 prepare split, moving to a new round, refusal of duplicate reports and two votes, preservation of previously emitted commit votes when a certificate is withheld, tampered view report, refusal of obsolete-round commits and hybrid signature requirements. No persistent round locks, process separation, leader election/pacemaker, timeout policy, multi-height ledger integration, formal safety proof, exhaustive adversarial model checking, physical tests or regulatory certification. Never substitute this for the existing fail-closed persistent ledger.
