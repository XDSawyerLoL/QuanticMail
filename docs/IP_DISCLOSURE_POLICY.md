# Intellectual property and disclosure policy

This public repository is not a confidential invention notebook.

Before publishing a new technical mechanism, assess whether it is a conventional engineering change or a potential invention. Keep the latter in access-controlled records outside public repositories, issues, CI artifacts and public demonstrations until reviewed by the rights holder and qualified intellectual-property counsel. Do not include undisclosed technical details in public searches or issue descriptions.

Maintain a confidential dated record of the technical problem, prior art, distinguishing features, experimental evidence, human inventive contributions, ownership and dependencies. Review novelty, inventive step, industrial applicability and freedom to operate separately. A dated record is evidence, not a patent. A provisional filing is not a granted patent. Patent status and dates must be supported by official records. Audit historical public disclosures before filing; making a repository private does not undo disclosure.

Use public repositories only for conventional changes or material explicitly cleared for disclosure. This policy is advisory until repository access and release controls have been configured by the owner. No patentability claim or filing is made by this document.
