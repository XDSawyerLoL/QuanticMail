# Quantic Pay — isolated laboratory v0.1
Shared local simulator for /pay (static Hostinger /mail/pay/) and packaged Windows dashboard. Explicit fictional recipients, integer cents, prepare/confirm/cancel/expiry, duplicate confirmation without second debit, test-only history in memory. No network, banking identifiers, real money, redemption, payment token, wallet keys or claim of official compatibility. No code changes to Identity Vault or Quantic Mail transport. A simulation receipt is not evidence of payment. Future work: define a PSP integration boundary and compare official pilot candidate ISO 20022/API/CPACE specifications, establish regulated partner, implement authorization with applicable SCA and financial-purpose binding separately from mail signatures, independent transaction integrity/reconciliation, privacy and audit. The BCE pilot FAQ (updated 28 October 2026) describes technical service providers supporting participating PSPs, with PSP responsibility: https://www.ecb.europa.eu/euro/digital_euro/pilot/html/ecb.faq-digital-euro-pilot.en.html. No participation or approval currently established.

Keys and proof state are process-local; no guarantee against compromised process or physical custody attack. Do not treat this as strong customer authentication or digital euro compliance.

V0.4 second increment: read-only synthetic proof-set reconciliation. Verify each historical P-256 + ML-DSA-65 signature against independently pinned Pay keys, canonical nonce-sorted commitment, reject duplicate nonces, then compare count and digest against at least three matching independently pinned Ed25519 checkpoint votes from four distinct witnesses. V0.4 third increment: separate local quorum simulation workflow, accessible in the Windows dashboard. The new flow checks a fresh 3/4 proof-set quorum before requesting the two test signatures, verifies the exact candidate intent and nonce, submits it to witnesses and requires a second fresh matching quorum over the old set plus the candidate before reducing the fictional balance. Any failure once the operation starts locks the session, with no automatic retry. The demo has ephemeral Pay keys and four separately held in-memory witness states; it is NOT the native Pay vault path, persistent independent authority deployment, consensus or atomic commit. Tests cover two successful three-witness transactions, pre/post quorum insufficiency and 2/2 split. Existing local native-vault simulation remains separate and is not quorum-protected. Real payment and settlement flags always false.
